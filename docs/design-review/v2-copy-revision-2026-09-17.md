## Editorial principle

Keep the Foundation's own terms where they carry meaning—self-realization, direct experience, balanced-state practice and service—and explain them through the teaching or activity concerned. Use the homepage to introduce; use inner pages to develop the subject. Avoid replacing useful explanations with interchangeable invitations to explore or connect.
